Electric power grids are facing unprecedented load growth from large-scale data centers and electrification of heating, transportation, and industrial loads, while simultaneously experiencing increasing outages because of extreme weather events and aging infrastructure. This is driving up costs as utilities invest in new generation capacity and grid resilience measures. These pressures are creating growing populations that experience energy poverty (lack of reliable access due to infrastructure failures) and energy insecurity (inability to pay for essential energy needs). Addressing these challenges requires integrating end-user priorities into power system planning, operation, and maintenance: a critical gap in current practice. 

Holistic Approaches to Power and ENergy (HAPPEN) is an energy systems research group with a focus on developing technology-driven solutions to ensure reliable and affordable access to sustainable energy, grounded in community-identified needs. Our research bridges power systems research with user-centric translational approaches by drawing on tools from optimization and control theory, hardware prototyping, field deployment, and community-engaged scholarship.
