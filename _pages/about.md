Hello! I'm an incoming Assistant Professor (starting Sep 2027) in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Massachusetts Amherst. I will be leading the **Holistic Approaches to Power and ENergy (HAPPEN) group** at UMass Amherst. I envision my research group addressing real-world challenges in power and energy systems using tools such as optimization and control, modeling and simulation, hardware prototyping and field deployment, and community-engagement. 

I am currently a postdoctoral modeling engineer at the National Laboratory of the Rockies (previously known as the National Renewable Energy Laboratory). I was previously a Pathways Fellow and Lecturer at UMass Amherst (2025-26). I have a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering and an MS in Electrical Engineering from the University of Wisconsin-Madison and a B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from the National Institute of Technology Karnataka, Surathkal, India.


**JOIN**
======

My research group will draw upon tools such as optimization, control, numerical simulations, power electronics, PCB design, embedde systems, community engagement, and field deployment. I do not expect a given project to involve all the tools. I expect prospective students to have experience in at least one but not all of these tools, and a strong interest in energy systems research.
